# RTS POS

## Register

product

## Users

Arabic-speaking owners, cashiers, supervisors, and inventory staff across retail,
supermarkets, clothing stores, restaurants, and coffee shops. They use RTS POS on
Windows throughout the workday and need fast, low-error sales, stock, customer,
supplier, and reporting workflows. The interface must remain useful under time
pressure, with scanners, thermal printers, cash drawers, and intermittent internet.

## Product Purpose

RTS POS is the point-of-sale product from Royal Technology Solutions. It brings
checkout, inventory, receipts, returns, customers, loyalty, suppliers, restaurant
tables, and business reporting into one dependable desktop application. Success
means a cashier can complete common sales without hesitation, owners can trust the
records, existing ADA POS customers retain their data during the rebrand, and future
updates install without disruption.

## Brand Personality

Confident, precise, approachable. RTS POS should feel engineered for real work:
professional without being corporate, modern without being unfamiliar, and polished
without distracting a cashier from the transaction.

## Anti-references

Avoid glassmorphism, decorative gradients, floating orbs, oversized marketing
headlines, novelty controls, emoji as the primary icon system, hidden labels, and
low-contrast gray interfaces. Do not imitate generic admin templates or turn
high-frequency actions into decorative cards. Never sacrifice transaction speed or
data density to make the product look like a landing page.

## Design Principles

- Make the next operational action obvious on every screen.
- Keep checkout fast, dense, and keyboard/scanner friendly.
- Use one consistent component and status language across every workflow.
- Express RTS through disciplined geometry and royal purple, not decoration.
- Preserve existing customer data, settings, and upgrade continuity throughout the rebrand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, logical tab order, reduced-motion
support, semantic labels, and status communication that does not rely on color alone.
Arabic RTL is the primary experience; numbers, codes, prices, and technical strings
must remain correctly readable. Controls must remain usable at 1024 x 700 and above.
